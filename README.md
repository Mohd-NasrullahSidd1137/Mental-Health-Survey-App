# 🧠 Mental Health in Tech Survey Analysis

An interactive data analytics project that explores mental health experiences, treatment-seeking behavior, and workplace attitudes among employees in the technology sector.

The project includes **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** using Python and an interactive **Streamlit dashboard** for data visualization and business insights.

---

## 🚀 Live Dashboard

### 🔗 Streamlit App

👉 https://mental-health-survey-app-1137.streamlit.app/

The interactive dashboard allows users to explore mental health treatment patterns, workplace support, demographic factors, and geographic trends using dynamic filters and visualizations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Mental health is an important aspect of a healthy workplace environment. This project analyzes survey responses to understand patterns related to mental health treatment, workplace support, employee attitudes, and access to mental health resources.

The analysis focuses on identifying relationships between mental health treatment and factors such as:

- Gender
- Family history of mental illness
- Work interference
- Company size
- Remote work
- Mental health benefits
- Care options
- Wellness programs
- Anonymity and confidentiality
- Medical leave
- Supervisor support
- Observed workplace consequences
- Geographic location

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Mental health conditions can affect employees' workplace experiences, productivity, and overall well-being. However, employees may face barriers such as lack of awareness, limited access to resources, confidentiality concerns, and workplace stigma.

The objective of this project is to analyze survey data and identify patterns in mental health treatment-seeking behavior and workplace attitudes within the technology sector.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze mental health treatment-seeking patterns.
- Understand how workplace factors relate to treatment behavior.
- Examine the relationship between family history and treatment.
- Analyze mental health support and benefits provided by organizations.
- Explore workplace attitudes toward mental health.
- Identify geographic patterns in the survey responses.
- Generate actionable insights that can help organizations improve mental health support systems.
